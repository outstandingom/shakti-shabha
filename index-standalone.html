Revolution
Mardini

Self-defense. Lathi-Kaushal. Sword training. Village camps across Maharashtra and beyond. The revolution that begins in the body and spreads to the spirit.

02

Counseling. Anonymized case studies. Breaking silence on trafficking, PTSD, puberty, and trauma. The safe sanctuary where survivors discover their inner warrior.
